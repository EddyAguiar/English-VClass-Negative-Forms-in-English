📘 1. Negation in the Present — do not / does not

Para negar ações no presente simples, usamos do not (don't) ou does not (doesn't) + verbo na forma base.

SubjectFormExampleÁudio
I / You / We / They do not (don't) I don't eat meat.
He / She / It does not (doesn't) She doesn't drive.
They do not (don't) They don't agree.
💡 Important: After don't / doesn't, use the base verb (no -s).
✅ She doesn't drive. ❌ She doesn't drives.
1️⃣ Negating Present Actions A1–A2
❓ How do we negate actions in the present?
✅ Use do not (don't) or does not (doesn't).
Examples: I don't eat meat. / She doesn't drive.
📚 Key Vocabulary: 🔊 don't → não (contração) 🔊 doesn't → não (3ª pessoa)

📘 2. Negation in the Past — did not

Para negar ações no passado simples, usamos did not (didn't) + verbo na forma base (não importa se o verbo é regular ou irregular).

SubjectFormExampleÁudio
All subjects did not (didn't) He didn't go to work yesterday.
All subjects did not (didn't) They didn't finish the project.
All subjects did not (didn't) I didn't see her at the party.
💡 Important: After didn't, use the base verb — never the past form.
✅ He didn't go. ❌ He didn't went.
2️⃣ Negating Past Actions A2
❓ How do we negate actions in the past?
✅ Use did not (didn't) + base verb.
Example: He didn't go to work yesterday.
📚 Key Vocabulary: 🔊 didn't → não (passado)

📘 3. Negation in the Future — will not · cannot

Para o futuro, usamos will not (won't) + verbo base. Para expressar incapacidade, usamos cannot (can't).

TypeFormExampleÁudio
Future will not (won't) + base verb They won't attend the meeting.
Future will not (won't) + base verb I won't forget your birthday.
Ability cannot (can't) + base verb She can't swim.
Ability cannot (can't) + base verb I cannot understand this.
💡 Attention: "Cannot" is written as one word; "can't" is the contraction. "Will not" contracts to "won't" (not "willn't").
3️⃣ Negating Future Actions A2
❓ How do we negate actions in the future?
✅ Use will not (won't) + base verb.
Example: They won't attend the meeting.
📚 Key Vocabulary: 🔊 won't → não (futuro)
4️⃣ Expressing Inability A2
❓ How do we express inability?
✅ Use cannot or can't.
Example: She can't swim.
📚 Key Vocabulary: 🔊 can't → não poder 🔊 cannot → não poder (formal)

📘 4. No × Not · Never

A diferença entre no e not é fundamental: "no" vem antes de substantivo; "not" vem com verbo.

WordUseExampleÁudio
No Before a noun (zero quantity) No problem. / There are no people here.
Not With verbs (negates the action/state) I am not ready. / She is not coming.
Never At no time; not ever I have never visited Japan.
5️⃣ 'Never' — Meaning A2–B1
❓ What does 'never' mean?
✅ Never means "nunca" / "at no time".
Example: I have never visited Japan.
📚 Key Vocabulary: 🔊 never → nunca
6️⃣ 'No' vs 'Not' A2
❓ What is the difference between 'no' and 'not'?
✅ No = before a noun (No problem). Not = with verbs (I am not ready).
Examples: No problem. / I am not ready.
📚 Key Vocabulary: 🔊 no → nenhum / zero 🔊 not → não

📘 5. Negative Words — nobody, nothing, none

Palavras negativas substituem sujeito ou objeto, eliminando a necessidade de "not".

WordMeaningExampleÁudio
Nobody / No one no person Nobody came to the event.
Nothing no thing I saw nothing.
Nowhere no place We went nowhere last weekend.
None not one / not any None of them came.
No + noun zero quantity There are no people here.
💡 Avoid double negatives: Choose either a negative word or "not" — never both.
✅ I saw nothing. / I didn't see anything. ❌ I didn't see nothing.
7️⃣ nobody, nothing, nowhere A2–B1
❓ How do we use 'nobody', 'nothing', etc.?
✅ They substitute the subject or object and remove the need for "not".
Examples: Nobody came to the event. / I saw nothing.
📚 Key Vocabulary: 🔊 nobody → ninguém 🔊 nothing → nada 🔊 nowhere → nenhum lugar 🔊 none → nenhum

📘 6. Advanced Negation — neither…nor · hardly · negative verbs

Formas mais sofisticadas de negação, essenciais para B1–B2.

8️⃣ neither...nor B1–B2
❓ What does 'neither...nor...' mean?
✅ Neither…nor = "nem…nem" — expressa dois negativos juntos.
Example: He drinks neither tea nor coffee.
📚 Key Vocabulary: 🔊 neither → nem um nem outro 🔊 nor → nem
9️⃣ hardly / barely B1–B2
❓ How do we use words like 'hardly', 'barely'?
✅ Hardly / barely = "quase não".
Examples: She hardly slept. / I barely passed.
📚 Key Vocabulary: 🔊 hardly → quase não 🔊 barely → mal / quase não
🔟 Negative verbs — deny, refuse, lack B1–B2
❓ Which verbs have negative meaning?
✅ Deny, refuse, lack express negative meaning even without "not".
Examples: He denied it. / They refused the offer. / They lack resources.
📚 Key Vocabulary: 🔊 deny → negar 🔊 refuse → recusar 🔊 lack → faltar

🔄 Question Tags — a forma negativa mais comum na conversação

Quando a frase principal é afirmativa, a tag é negativa; quando a frase principal é negativa, a tag é afirmativa.

Main sentenceTagExampleÁudio
Affirmative negative tag You are Brazilian, aren't you?
Negative affirmative tag She doesn't like coffee, does she?
Negative affirmative tag They won't come, will they?

🎧 9. Listening Practice — A Busy Week

Ouça o texto e responda às perguntas. Ele usa as principais formas negativas da aula em contexto.

Hi, my name is Bruno. I don't usually have a lot of free time during the week. I work in an office, but I don't like waking up early. My sister doesn't drive, so she takes the bus. Last weekend we didn't go anywhere — we stayed home. We didn't see anyone, and we didn't do anything special. Next week, I won't work on Friday because it's a holiday. I can't cook very well, so we'll probably order food. I have never been to Japan, but I would love to go one day. Nobody in my family speaks Japanese. Neither my brother nor I can speak it. I hardly ever watch TV, but I barely missed the news last night. Life isn't always easy, but we don't complain.
Olá, meu nome é Bruno. Eu normalmente não tenho muito tempo livre durante a semana. Trabalho num escritório, mas não gosto de acordar cedo. Minha irmã não dirige, então ela pega o ônibus. No fim de semana passado não fomos a lugar nenhum — ficamos em casa. Não vimos ninguém e não fizemos nada especial. Na próxima semana, não trabalharei na sexta porque é feriado. Não sei cozinhar muito bem, então provavelmente pediremos comida. Eu nunca fui ao Japão, mas adoraria ir um dia. Ninguém na minha família fala japonês. Nem meu irmão nem eu sabemos falar. Eu quase nunca assisto TV, mas quase perdi o jornal ontem à noite. A vida nem sempre é fácil, mas não reclamamos.

Listening Questions

1. Does Bruno usually have a lot of free time?

2. Does Bruno like waking up early?

3. Why does his sister take the bus?

4. Did they go anywhere last weekend?

5. Did they see anyone last weekend?

6. Will Bruno work next Friday?

7. Can Bruno cook very well?

8. Has Bruno ever been to Japan?

9. Who speaks Japanese in his family?

10. Does he watch TV a lot?
